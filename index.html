The Ultimate SHP builder is created by the Claude AI extension in VS Code. It is AI-created, but it has a large range of capabilities and is extremely easy to use.

Capabilities include:

  • Pixel and frame copying.
  • The ability to open SHP's.
  • New SHP's can be made in this builder.
  • SHP's can be be opened in one pane and another can be opened in another pane. Pieces of or all of either SHP's can be copied and drug into the other pane.
  • Uibits (faction flags, loading screens, etc.) can be opened and modified
  • All five palletes: Red Alert 1, Red Alert 2, Yuri's Revenge, Tiberian Dawn, and Tiberian Sun.
  • When copying, only real pixels are copied, no empty space.
  • Automatic centering

Here is how to use the Ultimate SHP Builder:

  • To make a new SHP, go to file, then new SHP. Tell it what you are building and the dimensions, and you're set.
  • To open an SHP, go to file, then Open SHP.
  • To change the pallete, click on the box with Tiberian Dawn in it. Palletes are Tiberian Dawn, Red Alert 1, Tiberian Sun, Red Alert 2, and Yuri's Revenge.
  • To paint, click "P."
  • To Erase, click "E."
  • To do click the find-out-what-color-it-is-by-clicking, click "I."
  • To select, clcik "S."
  • To Copy and paste or select and move, select with the select tool the area you want copied or moved. press ctrl+C and then ctrl+V to copy and paste.
  • To change the direction of the SHP, go to "edit" and click one of the rotate or flip options (very limited function).
  • To move the whole image around in a circle, select all of it, move it a little to trigger the move handle. Click and hold the small orange handle and move in a circle. Enter to stamp.
  • To make a new uibit, go to tools and select "uibit creator." Everything is like the SHP creator, except no palletes and no turning (not needed). Just start drawing the uibit. To find where the uibit lies on the page (needed for telling the yaml code in OpenRA mods), just select the uibit with the select tool and it will tell you where it lies.
  • To center the contents of the frame, just click the "Center" button.
  • There is a function where the you can replace colors in the SHP. Click on the "Color Replace" button, then add all the colors you want replaced and the colors you want to replace them with. You can replace those colors in all frames or just the one you have selected.
  • You open an SHP in a new pane and copy from the second SHP and drag it into the other pane. Fairly new feature, not thoroughly tested.
