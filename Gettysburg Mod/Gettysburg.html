Gettysburg Advanced

Gettysburg Advanced is a mod for Ultimate Generals: Gettysburg.

Installation instructions:

Click the gear button when underneath the main header Ultimate Generals Gettysburg in Steam. Click on manage, then browse local files. Go to Ultimate General Gettysburg_data, and replace Assembly-CSharp.dll with the one in the download.

Click on the green "code" button and click download zip.
